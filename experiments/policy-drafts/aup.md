# Acceptable Use Policy

> **Draft, not legal advice.** Written for Ekido ticket A4 and reviewed by Piyush. A lawyer must review it before launch. Items marked **[TO CONFIRM]** are decisions the company has not made yet.

*Version: draft 1 · Last updated: 19 November 2026*

Ekido helps you train a small AI model on your own examples and run it. Because anyone can use it, we have a few firm rules about what it must not be used for. They are written to be read, not to trap you.

## 1. What this applies to

This policy applies to everything you do on Ekido: the examples you upload, the instructions you give the assistant, the models you train, and the endpoints and API keys you use to run them.

## 2. What you must not use Ekido for

You must not use Ekido to build, train, host or run a model, or to upload data, that is meant to:

1. **Harm computers or accounts.** Malware, ransomware, spyware, tools that break into systems or steal passwords, or help hide such tools from security software.
2. **Watch or track a person without their knowledge.** Following someone's location, reading their private messages, or building a profile of a named person to stalk or pressure them.
3. **Harass or threaten.** Abusive or threatening messages, including sending them in bulk or from many numbers to get around blocks.
4. **Collect private data.** Scraping people's private accounts, messages, phone numbers or emails, or collecting personal data without a lawful reason and, where required, consent.
5. **Deceive people.** Fake reviews, fake testimonials, fake investment or loan schemes, phishing, impersonating a person, bank or company, or content meant to defraud.
6. **Fake or defeat identity checks.** Forged Aadhaar, PAN or other identity documents, or anything built to get past KYC or to avoid law enforcement.
7. **Create sexual or abusive images.** Any sexual content involving children, and any non-consensual intimate or "deepfake" content of a real person.
8. **Send spam.** Mass unsolicited messages, or models whose purpose is spam or fraud.
9. **Break the law.** Anything that is illegal in India or in the place where you or the people affected live.

## 3. Data you upload

- Upload only data you have the right to use. Do not upload other people's personal data unless you have a lawful reason to hold it.
- Remove personal details where you can. Ekido removes common ones (such as phone numbers, emails, Aadhaar and PAN numbers) in your browser before upload, but this removal is not perfect and does not replace your own care.
- Do not upload data that is illegal to hold, such as child sexual abuse material.

## 4. How we enforce it

- **At the start.** The assistant that sets up jobs refuses requests that clearly fall under section 2. Only recipes on our approved list can be trained. This screening is automatic and can be wrong in both directions; if you think a request was refused by mistake, contact us (see section 6).
- **Afterwards.** We may review a job or an endpoint if we receive a credible report, if a payment is disputed, or if we see signs of abuse such as sudden heavy use or reports from others. We do not routinely read customers' data.
- **What we may do.** Depending on how serious it is: warn you, stop a job, switch off an endpoint or API key, suspend or close an account, refuse a refund for a job that broke this policy, and pass information to the authorities where the law requires it or to prevent serious harm.
