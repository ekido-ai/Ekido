## 5. The quality guarantee and refunds

For a per-job purchase, the quote states a quality bar before you pay. A model **passes** only if both are true:
1. it reaches the minimum score for the recipe on **your own test examples**, and
2. it does clearly better than the same base model before training.

If the model does not pass, **you are refunded automatically** in full for that job, to the way you paid, and devices are paid only for work that passed. Refunds can take a few working days to reach your bank.

The guarantee is about the measured score on your test examples. It does not promise how the model behaves on data unlike your examples, and it does not cover a job you started with data that is empty, wrongly labelled or too small to learn from. We tell you in the quote and in the result what was measured. **[TO CONFIRM: the minimum data we require before a quote]**
