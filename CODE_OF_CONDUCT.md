# Code of conduct

We want GPUChain to be a friendly place to learn and build, for everyone.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and answer questions patiently.
- Credit other people's work.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Pushing anyone to use real funds, or sharing private keys.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block people who repeatedly do so. To report a problem, contact a maintainer privately through GitHub.
